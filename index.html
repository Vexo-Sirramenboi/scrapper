GameHub

Link checker for networks using Cisco Umbrella (DNS filtering). Paste your links and mirrors, hit Check all — it probes every site from your network and shows what's reachable right now.

How it works / limits
Umbrella filters at the DNS layer: blocked domains fail to resolve (NXDOMAIN) or answer with a block page. This page tests each link with a real browser request, so a green result means the domain resolves and responds on this network right now.

• Blocked or down — DNS/connection failed. Could be Umbrella, or a dead mirror.
• Reachable — verify — the site answered, but its favicon didn't load, which can happen when Umbrella is serving its block page instead of the real site. Open it manually to confirm.
• Keep 2–3 mirrors per game, because domains rotate and get re-blocked constantly.
• If your school also blocks github.io itself or uses a deep TLS-inspection proxy (not just DNS), this approach won't help.
